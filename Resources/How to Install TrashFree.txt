How to install TrashFree
========================

TrashFree is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to approve it the first
time you open it. You only do this once.

1. Drag TrashFree into your Applications folder.

2. Open TrashFree. macOS shows a dialog titled "TrashFree" Not Opened.
   Click Done. Don't click Move to Bin.

3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and scroll down to Security.
   Next to "TrashFree" was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway.
   Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open.

That's it. From now on TrashFree opens like any other app.

Before the first scan, TrashFree asks for Full Disk Access so it can measure every
folder. You can say no; it then scans what it can reach.

Pictures of each step: https://antedotee.github.io/trashfree/#install
